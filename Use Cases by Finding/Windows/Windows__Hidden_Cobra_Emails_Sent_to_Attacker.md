# Data Exfiltration to Hidden Cobra Controlled Email

## Description
Alert generated by the `Data Exfiltration to Hidden Cobra Controlled Email` detection. Detects emails sent from the organization to external addresses linked to the Hidden Cobra threat group. This activity is a direct indicator of data exfiltration, where a compromised host or an insider sends sensitive information, documents, or credentials directly to the attacker's infrastructure using legitimate mail protocols. The alert must be reviewed by the SOC to validate its legitimacy, scope, affected asset, and potential impact.

## Objective
Detect and prioritize potential malicious, anomalous, or unauthorized activity related to Windows host, enabling the SOC to initiate a structured investigation, reduce operational risk, and activate containment procedures when indicators of compromise are identified.

## Logic
The detection is triggered by the `Data Exfiltration to Hidden Cobra Controlled Email` rule. The prioritization logic should consider severity, asset criticality, event recurrence, network source/destination, involved identity, platform, and correlated activity in supporting data sources.

Patterns or conditions to validate:

- Exact match of the detection name/signature with this use case.
- Severity equal to or above the SOC-defined threshold.
- Production, critical, publicly exposed, or sensitive-data asset.
- IP address, domain, user, process, or resource not recognized by the asset/account owner.
- Logs or telemetry from the supporting data sources occurring close to the first or last observed event.
- Sensitive changes following the event, such as permission changes, new persistence, credential use, logging changes, public exposure, command execution, or data access.

## Investigation

- **Who:** Which user, host, service account, process, or external actor is involved?
- **What:** What action was observed, and what asset was affected? Was there access, execution, a configuration change, network communication, or an authentication attempt?

## Evidence for Determination

### False Positives

- The `Data Exfiltration to Hidden Cobra Controlled Email` rule has not yet been tuned to this environment and is matching a legitimate, documented pattern - confirm with the asset/account owner before escalating.
- An approved backup, replication, or data-sharing job legitimately transfers data at a similar volume or destination.
- An approved backup, reporting, or data-processing job legitimately collects or stages the data as part of its documented function.

### True Positives

- This activity is a direct indicator of data exfiltration, where a compromised host or an insider sends sensitive information, documents, or credentials directly to the attacker's infrastructure using legitimate mail protocols.
- The transfer targets a destination or volume with no legitimate business relationship to the source, and follows a collection or credential-theft indicator.
- The transfer uses a channel (DNS, email, an unusual protocol) chosen specifically to blend in or evade monitored egress paths.
- The collection targets sensitive data with no legitimate business relationship to the collecting identity or process.
- It is followed by staging or exfiltration-related activity.

### True Negatives

- The transfer is confirmed to be an approved, documented data movement.
- The activity matches an approved, documented data-handling process.

### False Negatives

- Data is exfiltrated slowly, in small volumes, over a long period to stay under volumetric thresholds.
- The channel used is not currently monitored by this detection's data sources.
- Collection is performed slowly and narrowly enough to resemble normal application usage.
